ZERϴ Network has shut down. Your XP and your Level were kept, and you still earn XP in Zerion Rewards. This article explains what changed and what did not.
What changed
Daily Earnings now come from your whole wallet.
Before the shutdown, you earned XP every day for the assets you held on ZERϴ Network. Now you earn XP every day based on your balance across all Zerion-supported chains.
The rate is different.
An estimated $2,000 worth of assets earns you 1 XP per day. Balance above $1,000,000 does not earn extra XP.
Boosted Tokens are gone.
No token or pool earns extra XP anymore. Every asset counts at the same rate.
What stayed the same
Your XP and Level.
XP you earned before the shutdown still counts toward your Level.
Quests, Gasback and Referrals.
They work the same way as before.

Do you need to do anything?
