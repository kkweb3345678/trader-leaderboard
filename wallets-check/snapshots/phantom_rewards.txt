Refer friends and earn with Phantom
The Phantom Referral Program lets you invite friends and earn 25% of the net trading fees Phantom receives from their eligible trades for three months. Your friends get 10% of their own eligible trading fees back for three months.
Earnings and rebates are paid in CASH to each person’s Cash balance.
Referral codes can only be applied during signup in the Phantom mobile app. They can’t be applied when signing up through the browser extension or Phantom Terminal. Once a friend signs up through a referral on mobile, their eligible swaps on the browser extension also count toward referral benefits.

Sign up with a referral code
#
To receive referral benefits as a new user, sign up in the Phantom mobile app using a valid, active referral link or QR code, or enter a referral code during signup.
Enter a referral code when creating your Phantom wallet
Check that the code appears during signup. If it hasn’t been added automatically, enter it manually on the referral screen.
Referral codes can’t be added after signup.
What referred users receive
#
As a referred user, you can receive 10% of the fees paid on your own eligible trades back as a rebate. The rebate goes to your Cash balance. It isn’t deducted from the trading fee upfront.
Your referral information appears in the fee breakdown when you confirm an eligible trade
Phantom shows the rebate percentage at transaction confirmation. Your three-month rebate period starts when the referral code is applied to your new account during mobile signup. The app doesn’t currently show the expiry date or how much time remains.
When your rebate period ends, the “10% discount on fees” message stops appearing during checkout. The period may end earlier if the program or your eligibility ends.
Rates and earning windows may change.
What referrers earn
#
You earn 25% of the net trading fees Phantom receives from each referred user’s eligible trades. You begin earning once your friend funds their wallet and completes their first eligible trade.
Each referral’s three-month earning period starts when your code is applied to your friend’s new account during mobile signup. Only eligible trades within that period count. You won’t earn a fee share on trades where Phantom receives no net trading fee.
How payments work
#
Referral earnings and rebates are paid in CASH to each recipient’s Cash balance. Payments are made at most once per day, after the recipient meets the $1 minimum payout threshold.
Before receiving payment, you may also need to complete identity verification, provide requested information, or maintain an eligible Cash account.
Eligible trades
#
Spot trades, perps, and predictions qualify for referral benefits. Each trade must take place within the applicable rebate or earning window.

If you haven’t received a payout
#
Check the following:
The referred user signed up through a valid, active referral link or entered the code during signup.
The trade qualified for the program and took place within the applicable rebate or earning window.
If you’re the referrer, your friend funded their wallet and completed their first eligible trade.
You’ve met the applicable payout threshold and completed any requested verification.
Referrers won’t earn a fee share on trades where Phantom receives no net trading fee.
