Refer friends with your code and earn from their trades
Invite friends to Phantom and earn a share of the net trading fees Phantom receives from their eligible trades. Your friends get a rebate on their own eligible trading fees.
The standard rates are
25% for referrers
and
10% back for referred friends
, with benefits lasting three months. Earnings and rebates are paid in CASH to each person’s Cash balance.
Friends give, friends get: November 20–December 13, 2026
#
During the Friends give, friends get campaign, eligible referrals receive higher rates:
Referrers earn 35%
of the net trading fees Phantom receives from their friends’ eligible trades, instead of 25%.
Referred friends receive 15% back
on their own eligible trading fees, instead of 10%.
The rebate is paid to your Cash balance. It isn’t deducted from the trading fee upfront.
The campaign rates apply only to eligible trades made from November 20 through December 13, 2026. After December 13, the standard rates apply for the remainder of each referral’s three-month benefit period. Joining near the end of the campaign doesn’t extend the higher rates.
The campaign isn’t available to UK users. US predictions trades don’t qualify for referral benefits.

Sign up with a referral code
#
To receive referral benefits as a new user, sign up in the Phantom mobile app using a valid, active referral link or QR code, or enter a referral code during signup.
Enter your referral code at wallet creation
Check that the code appears during signup. If it hasn’t been added automatically, enter it manually on the referral screen.
Referral codes can’t be added after signup.
They also can’t be applied when signing up through the browser extension or Phantom Terminal.
Once you sign up through a referral on mobile, your eligible swaps on the browser extension also count toward referral benefits.
What referred users receive
#
As a referred user, you can receive a percentage of the fees paid on your own eligible trades back as a rebate. The standard rebate is 10%. During the
Friends give, friends get campaign
, the rebate is 15% on qualifying trades.
The rebate goes to your Cash balance as CASH. Phantom shows the rebate percentage in the fee breakdown when you confirm an eligible trade.
Your referral information appears in the fee breakdown when you confirm an eligible trade
Your three-month rebate period starts when the referral code is applied to your new account during mobile signup. The app doesn’t currently show the expiry date or how much time remains.
When your rebate period ends, the referral discount message stops appearing during checkout. The period may end earlier if the program or your eligibility ends.
Rates and earning windows may change.
What referrers earn
#
You earn a share of the net trading fees Phantom receives from each referred user’s eligible trades. The standard share is 25%. During the
Friends give, friends get campaign
, it is 35% on qualifying trades.
You begin earning once your friend funds their wallet and completes their first eligible trade.
Each referral’s three-month earning period starts when your code is applied to your friend’s new account during mobile signup. Only eligible trades within that period count. You won’t earn a fee share on trades where Phantom receives no net trading fee.
How payments work
#
Referral earnings and rebates are paid in CASH to each recipient’s Cash balance. Payments are made at most once per day, after the recipient meets the $1 minimum payout threshold.
Before receiving payment, you may also need to complete identity verification, provide requested information, or maintain an eligible Cash account.
Eligible trades
#
Spot trades, perps, and international predictions qualify for referral benefits, subject to program eligibility. US predictions trades are excluded.
Each trade must take place within the applicable rebate or earning window. To receive the
Friends give, friends get campaign
rates, the trade must also take place during the campaign period.
Track your referrals and rebates
#
Open the sidebar, then tap
Earn

A referral counts as successful when your friend completes their first eligible trade.
Phantom sends a notification when referral earnings or rebates are credited to your Cash balance.
If you haven’t received a payout
#
Check the following:
The referred user signed up through a valid, active referral link or entered the code during signup.
The trade qualified for the program and took place within the applicable rebate or earning window.
If you’re the referrer, your friend funded their wallet and completed their first eligible trade.
You’ve met the applicable payout threshold and completed any requested verification.
Referrers won’t earn a fee share on trades where Phantom receives no net trading fee.
